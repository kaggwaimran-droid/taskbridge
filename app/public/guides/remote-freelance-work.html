Remote Freelance Work and Online Projects

Connect your skills with remote work opportunities

TaskBridge is developing a global-minded marketplace for people offering skills and clients looking for help. The platform is intended to support work ranging from small assignments to professional services and longer projects.

Types of work

Potential categories include writing, translation, graphic design, video editing, web development, research, marketing, administration and virtual assistance. Availability depends on genuine client listings published on the marketplace.

How applications work

For professional services and larger projects, workers can review the brief and apply. The client reviews applications and chooses a worker before the work begins. Always agree on scope, deliverables and deadlines through the platform.

Important platform status

TaskBridge is an early-stage marketplace. Listings are not a promise of work or income, and payment processing and escrow are not yet active. Do not send money to strangers to secure a job.
