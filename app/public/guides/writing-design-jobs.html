Writing and Design Freelance Opportunities

Put your creative skills to work

TaskBridge aims to help clients find people for writing, translation, graphic design and other creative assignments. Workers can use a portfolio, clear proposals and relevant examples to explain how they can meet a client's brief.

Writing and language services

Possible assignments include proofreading, editing, product descriptions, articles, translation and copywriting. Always confirm the intended audience, word count, tone, language and revision expectations.

Design and creative services

Possible assignments include social graphics, presentations, simple brand assets and video editing. Confirm dimensions, file formats, source-file expectations and usage rights before accepting work.

Keep expectations clear

Only apply to genuine listings and respect copyright and client confidentiality. TaskBridge is an early-stage platform; listings and payments are not guaranteed and payment processing is not yet active.
