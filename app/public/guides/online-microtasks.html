Online Microtasks and Paid Online Tasks

Discover online microtasks on TaskBridge

TaskBridge is building a marketplace where clients can publish clearly described online tasks and workers can complete eligible assignments. Microtasks may include simple research, data categorisation, transcription, testing and other focused digital work.

How microtasks work

Workers browse available listings, review the instructions and reward, claim an eligible task, complete the work and submit proof for client review. Only work approved under the task instructions can qualify for earnings.

What to check before starting

Read the full task description, confirm the required proof and deadline, and never pay a person who promises to give you a job. TaskBridge payment processing is not yet active, so do not treat displayed examples or pending records as withdrawable money.
