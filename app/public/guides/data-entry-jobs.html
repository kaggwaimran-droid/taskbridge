Online Data Entry and Research Tasks

Explore data entry and research work

TaskBridge is designed to connect clients who need organised information with workers who can complete focused digital assignments. Examples include spreadsheet formatting, data categorisation, online research and document organisation.

Skills that can help

Careful typing, spreadsheet basics, attention to detail, accurate source checking and clear communication can be useful for data-related assignments. Some client briefs may require specific software or experience.

Before you apply

Check the task instructions, expected format, source requirements, time estimate and proof needed for approval. Do not submit private customer data or information you are not authorised to handle.
